Web analytics privacy

We use Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.) to measure page views and clicks on download links. The analytics tracker does not set cookies. URL query parameters and fragments are excluded from analytics.

Umami processes pages viewed, referring pages, and information about the browser and device. It processes IP addresses to distinguish visits and approximate location. IP addresses are not stored in analytics records. Umami and its providers may record them in operational and security logs.

Analytics data is stored in the EU and retained for six months on the Hobby plan. See the Umami Privacy Policy for more information.
